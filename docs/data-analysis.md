### Quick Summary of Results
- **Full Dataset Size**: 43,071 sections across 1,467 Acts (56 volumes, 563 MB).
- **Language Breakdown**: 51.40% Bangla, 46.87% English, 1.56% Mixed/Bilingual.
- **Repeal Status**: 82.21% Active sections vs 17.79% Repealed sections.
- **Profiles Generated**:
  - `data/processed/bdlaws_profile.csv`
  - `data/processed/sample_profile.csv`
